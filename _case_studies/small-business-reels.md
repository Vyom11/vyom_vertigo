## The brief

Independent neighbourhood businesses—including an artisanal bakery and two other local retail establishments <!-- TODO(Vyom): name the industry of each, never the brand -->—needed punchy, authentic short-form video content to attract local footfall and drive digital engagement on community feeds without large agency budgets.

## The idea

Infuse everyday local retail moments with energetic comedic sketches, mouth-watering product highlights, and warm neighborhood personality that makes viewers feel like regulars before they even step through the door.

## What I did

- Performed on camera as the lead actor across episodic short-form video reels.
- Provided hands-on direction support on set to optimize camera movement, physical comedy, and dynamic visual hooks.
- Refined script delivery and improvised natural dialogue tailored to each shop's local ambiance and customer base.

## How it worked

Small businesses thrive on familiarity and charm. Putting expressive, high-energy storytelling at the heart of their feeds differentiated them from stale static photo promotions and captured attention in regional explore feeds.

## The results

- Reels across the three local businesses crossed **200k+** views combined.
- Drove sustained community comments, shares, and local customer recognition.
