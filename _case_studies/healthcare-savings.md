## The brief

A dedicated savings-focused medicine sub-brand of an established regional diagnostics enterprise was seeking to articulate its core value proposition clearly and humanely, without falling into the clinical detachment, fear-based warnings, or impenetrable pharmaceutical terminology that dominates medical advertising.

The brand's stated core promise centered on offering 60% savings on diabetic, cardiac, and chronic-disease prescription medicines.

The challenge was sensitive: address a deeply vulnerable, recurring monthly financial burden faced by elderly patients and working-class caregivers, while maintaining strict adherence to regulatory boundaries—making zero unverified medical claims and ensuring all communication remained reassuring, honest, and accessible.

## The idea

Transform recurring monthly medicine expenses from a source of perpetual anxiety into an empowering household budgeting relief, framed through empathetic, authentic everyday scenarios rather than sterile clinical demonstrations.

Rather than portraying illness, we focused on the peace of mind that comes when life-saving chronic treatments no longer strain the family grocery and education budget. Through warm, conversational monologues, on-camera authenticity, and clear mathematical infographics, we positioned the sub-brand not as a distant pharmacy, but as an empathetic ally to every household managing long-term care.

## What I did

- **Scriptwriting & Narrative Framing:** Wrote conversational, human-centric reel scripts that demystified prescription affordability and chronic care management without touching clinical claims or diagnoses.
- **On-Camera Performance as Lead Actor:** Performed as the primary on-screen face and presenter across video campaigns, bringing warmth, reassuring eye contact, and emotional accessibility to a subject that often feels sterile or frightening.
- **Creative Direction:** Directed the video shoots to ensure unhurried pacing, clear audio delivery, natural lighting, and an earnest, trustworthy emotional tone suitable for family audiences.
- **Carousel & Educational Copy:** Authored clear, swipeable social carousels translating pharmacy savings calculations into simple, intuitive visual comparisons that families could easily comprehend at a glance.

## How it worked

Healthcare marketing requires a delicate touch; any hint of aggressive commercialism or fear-mongering triggers immediate skepticism:

1. **Empathetic Everyday Scenarios:** By highlighting the everyday conversations between adult children and aging parents discussing monthly refill bills, the scripts established immediate emotional recognition.
2. **Strict Medical Restraint:** By framing affordability strictly as the brand's stated value promise—without offering unsolicited medical advice or interfering with physician authority—the campaign established credibility and regulatory compliance.
3. **Approachable Human Delivery:** Speaking directly into the lens with quiet confidence and empathetic timing made complex prescription budgeting feel manageable and transparent.

## The results

- Generated **20k+** likes and widespread organic shares across community health networks.
- Positioned the sub-brand as a compassionate, transparent option for families navigating chronic medical expenses.
