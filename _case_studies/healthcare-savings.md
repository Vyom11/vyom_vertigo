## The brief

A dedicated savings-focused medicine sub-brand of a regional diagnostics business needed to articulate its value proposition clearly without falling into medical jargon or clinical detachment. The brand's stated core promise is 60% savings on diabetic and chronic-disease medicines.

<!-- TODO(Vyom): confirm exact wording of the claim, e.g. "up to 60%". Present it as the brand's own claim, and make no medical claims -->

## The idea

Transform recurring monthly medicine expenses from an anxious, sterile medical transaction into an empowering household budgeting win, framed through empathetic, authentic everyday scenarios rather than fear-based messaging.

## What I did

- Wrote conversational reel scripts demystifying prescription affordability and chronic care management.
- Performed on camera as the lead actor in video reels to deliver reassuring, relatable storytelling.
- Directed the reel shoots to maintain clear pacing, relatable emotional tone, and high viewer retention.
- Authored informative social carousels translating pharmacy savings calculations into intuitive visual cards.

## How it worked

Long-term care patients and caregivers are often overwhelmed by recurring healthcare costs. By presenting the brand's affordability promise with warmth and clarity—while carefully steering clear of unverified medical claims—the campaign built rapport with families managing recurring medical budgets.

## The results

- Generated **20k+** likes across social campaigns.
