## What they're doing now

The [homepage](https://sleepyowl.co/) opens on an Xpresso banner ("the strongest coffee we ever made") with "Warning: Strong coffee" on the product. Cold Brew carries "Brew overnight & Smooooth AF", and the Filter Coffee line is "Undeniably Kaapi, unbelievably easy". The page also runs several offers and combos (free coffee with a French press, a mug pack) and lists quick-commerce availability. The [Protein Coffee page](https://sleepyowl.co/products/protein-coffee-classic) uses "Cold Coffee, Only Stronger!" and lists about 15 g of whey protein per sachet, caffeine stated as 119 mg, no added sugar (sweetened with sucralose) and a pack of 5 sachets. One line there says under 97 kcal and another says under 100.

The [blog](https://sleepyowl.co/blogs/news) lists nine posts, mostly guides, such as ["Everything You Need To Know About Arabica Coffee Beans"](https://sleepyowl.co/blogs/news/everything-you-need-to-know-about-arabica-coffee-beans) and ["Interesting Facts You Must Know About Cold Brew"](https://sleepyowl.co/blogs/news/interesting-facts-you-must-know-about-cold-brew). The page shows no dates.

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sleepyowlcoffee/) has about 90.7k followers and 192 posts, with a bio about "brewing revolution in a cup". The recent grid I could read shows three things. A **Sparkling Coffee launch** ran as a sequence: a teaser, a launch-tomorrow post, a "the wait is over" post, a character called Ayaan and a "coffee or cola?" 2 PM-crisis post. A [guess-the-flavour contest](https://www.instagram.com/sleepyowlcoffee/p/DfFDbZ2nZQ2/) ran 29 Oct to 1 Nov, with a month's supply for the first five correct guesses. And several **science explainers** cover why espresso hits hard, why coffee sends people to the loo and how grind size changes a cup, alongside a numbered "Day 11, 12, 13" series arguing instant coffee makes great cold coffee. (Captions and thumbnails only, truncated; I did not watch the reels.)

For history: in August 2022, [afaqs reported](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/sleepy-owl-launches-brand-mascot) a campaign built on "That Good" and "Your Loss", with a blue owl mascot, a "Fear of Missing Owl" twist, four films aimed at millennials and Gen Z, and a stated choice to avoid celebrity endorsements. In May 2025, co-branded Hocco ice-cream posts, [as indexed by Particl](https://www.particl.com/sleepy-owl-coffee/our-scooperheroes-met-@sleepyowlcoffee-1120337-54ba7358) (3 May 2025), included the caption "Chaos meets comfort." And the [Premium Instant launch](https://www.foodbev.com/news/sleepy-owl-expands-portfolio-with-premium-instant-coffee) (Jan 2022) described 100% Arabica with a mix of 95% soluble crystals and 5% freshly roasted ground coffee.

## The gap

Credit first. Of these five brands, Sleepy Owl's microcopy is the sharpest I read ("Smooooth AF" is a better product line than most brands manage in a whole deck), the mascot has a point of view, and the product ladder is clear, from instant to cold brew to protein.

So where does the owl go quiet? The blog. The feed has the voice and the blog doesn't. Instagram explainers are written with a wink (a joke about your bladder RSVPing before the coffee does), while the blog titles read like search-engine titles ("Everything You Need To Know About..."), a different person from the one writing "Warning: Strong coffee". The same material is working harder on the feed than on the surface that search actually finds. It's like casting your funniest actor in the trailer and handing the film to the understudy.

Second, promise versus ticker. The brand's stated posture in 2022 was to avoid overblown product messaging, and a homepage with many codes and combos pulls the other way. That's a reading of the page, not a verdict on the strategy.

Third, one number with two values. The protein page says both "under 97" and "under 100" calories. It's small, and it's the kind of thing a copy audit catches before a screenshot does.

## The refinement

**Move 1: Rewrite the blog in the owl's voice.**  
"Before" lines are real titles from the blog. "After" lines are proposals.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Differences Between Arabica Coffee Beans and Robusta Coffee Beans | Arabica vs Robusta: Two Beans, One Opinionated Owl |
| Interesting Facts You Must Know About Cold Brew | Cold Brew Is Not Iced Coffee. Say It With Us. |
| How To Make Coffee From Coffee Powder At Home Without Using A Machine | No machine. No problem. Still coffee. |

Sample lede for the second: *"Cold brew and iced coffee are different things. One of them is patient. The other is just cold."*

**Move 2: After the launch, a reason to reorder.**  
The Sparkling Coffee launch sequence did the teaser, the countdown and the reveal. This is a follow-up reel for the "2 PM crisis" it names. I haven't tasted it, so no product claims beyond coffee-and-cola.

- **Hook (0-3s):** an office desk at 2 PM. One hand reaches for a cola. The other hand reaches for a coffee. Text: *"2 baje. Coffee ya cola?"*
- **(3-9s):** the hands arm-wrestle. The owl, deadpan, drops a Sparkling Coffee between them.
- **(9-14s):** *(Beat.)* Both hands, sheepishly, retreat. Text: *"Dono. Bas, ek can mein."*
- **End card:** "Sleepy Owl Sparkling Coffee. Ab decide mat karo."

**Move 3: Extend the "Warning" device.**  
Xpresso already says "Warning: Strong coffee". A carousel of mock labels (jokes, not health claims):

> **Warning:** May cause sudden productivity.  
> **Warning:** Side effects include opinions before 9 am.  
> **Warning:** Strong coffee. (We did say.)

## How I'd measure it

All **hypotheses**; I have no Sleepy Owl baselines.

- **H1:** Voice-led blog titles lift click-through from search and social versus the current titles. Watch CTR, time on page and blog-to-product-page clicks.
- **H2:** A post-launch character reel earns more saves and shares than a repeat product shot, and lifts repeat visits to the Sparkling Coffee page. Watch saves, shares, link clicks and repeat-purchase rate.
- **H3:** The "Warning" carousel earns above-average share rates versus offer-led posts. Watch shares and follower growth per post.

## Sign-off

*Strong coffee. Stronger voice.*
