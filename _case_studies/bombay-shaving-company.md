## What they're doing now

The [homepage](https://bombayshavingcompany.com/) opens with "We've got your back BRO!" and the line "grooming for the bits nobody else sells you properly." It sells trimmers, razors (Switch4 among them), fragrances and skincare, with Hinglish scattered through ("Old school uncle left the chat") and a "Khul Ke Khel" banner.

The [Instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/bombayshavingcompany/) has about 406k followers and 2,507 posts. Its bio rhymes "jhijhak" with "vibe" and carries #KhulKeKhel, and the highlights include JHIJHAK, Science Ya Scam, Razorpreneur 2.0 and ShavesForGood. In the recent grid I could read, reel captions show a running "Uncle" character (a Children's Day greeting, a joke about a tech outage, a mock-defiant "I'm gonna do it anyway"), a Science Ya Scam episode on pheromones, a Diwali greeting, a Diwali giveaway on the perfume account and a conversation with a Marathi music act. (Captions and thumbnails only; I did not watch the reels.)

The campaigns that reached the trade press are a different set. [Styled by Bombay Shaving Company](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/bombay-shaving-company-unveils-styled-by-bombay-shaving-company-campaign) (Dec 2023) was a film for a new trimmer range, with founder and CEO Shantanu Deshpande in it. [Be Sensi](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/bombay-shaving-companys-ceo-shantanu-deshpande-leads-sensi-brigade-to-rescue-shavers-in-new-spot) had the founder lead a "Sensi Brigade" against razor burn for the Sensi Smart 3, with the agency Sideways credited with the concept. [#IndiaShavesItsStubble](https://www.medianews4u.com/bombay-shaving-companys-indiashavesitsstubble-campaign-invites-indians-to-turn-a-daily-ritual-into-a-mindful-revolution-with-conscious-shaving/) (Apr 2025) built a campaign around the Eco Sensi 3, a razor with a coconut-shell and rice-husk handle (launch pack Rs. 179), plus a short film titled "Where Do All the Razors Go?". It launched in an 18-hour online sale that repeats monthly on the 18th at 6 pm.

Then Switch4. The [ad used a mock courtroom](https://www.inventiva.co.in/?p=441853). On 17 September 2026 the Delhi High Court heard Gillette India's objection that it disparaged Gillette's products without naming them. BSC agreed to take the original down from platforms it controls, without admitting the allegations, and filed a revised version without the courtroom or Gillette references. Gillette objected to that too; the court neither approved nor banned it. Open magazine had framed the [campaign as BSC calling the old razor "Uncle"](https://openthemagazine.com/branding-marketing-and-advertising/bombay-shaving-company-vs-gillette-uncle-why-so-serious). Separately, BSC [appointed Enormous on 19 Feb 2026](https://manifest-media.in/advertising/190226/enormous-bags-creative-mandate-for-bombay-shaving-company.html), aiming for work on self-assurance, identity and limitlessness.

## The gap

Credit first. A founder who'll be the face of the brand is a real asset, the Hinglish sounds like a person and not a policy, and #IndiaShavesItsStubble came with a mechanic, not just a message.

But here's the thing about a borrowed villain. Putting an incumbent in the antagonist's chair gives you instant drama, and it also hands the story to the incumbent's lawyers. That's what happened: the dispute became the headline, and the original ad was taken down by agreement. A villain that can't sue is a better villain. A habit, say.

The second gap is that the spine lives in the feed, not in the campaigns. Uncle is already the brand's running foil on Instagram, sitting beside Science Ya Scam and #KhulKeKhel. The press-led campaigns (Sensi Brigade, Eco Sensi, Switch4) read like separate shows. The Enormous brief (self-assurance, identity, limitlessness) points at the same joining thread. That's a reading, not a fact.

Third, a ritual with no content around it. The monthly 18th-at-6pm sale is a built-in appointment, and in the sources I could read, no recurring content format surrounds it. It's a premiere with no trailer.

## The refinement

**Move 1: A habit-as-villain headline set for Switch4.**  
No price or performance claims. Those come from BSC's approved sheet.

> Your blade has been with you since college. That's not loyalty. That's inertia.  
> Switch the blade. Keep the swagger.  
> Purana blade ko alvida kaho.  
> Loyalty is for people. Blades get replaced.

**Move 2: Uncle, in a duel, not a courtroom.**  
The foil already exists on the feed. "Mere paas ___ hai" is the dialogue-duel, borrowed from Deewaar, with Uncle across the bathroom mirror.

- **Hook (0-2s):** black frame, the snap of a razor. Uncle, deadpan, to the mirror: *"Mere paas purana blade hai."*
- **(2-10s):** the young man across from him holds up a Switch4. Slow push-in. No music.
- **(10-20s):** quick cuts of the old blade's "greatest hits": the nick, the drag, the third use of the week. All played as a mock-tribute.
- **(20-25s):** reveal. Clean jaw, one raised eyebrow. Line: *"Habit chhodo. Swagger rakho."*  
*(Beat. Let the silence do the selling.)*

**Move 3: Make the 18th an appointment.**  
Built only on the mechanic in the launch coverage: monthly, 18th, 6 pm, 18 hours, website only.

- **Countdown reel hook:** *"Ek mahina. Ek tareekh. Atharah ghante."*
- **Carousel (5 slides):** "18 × 6 × 18" (slide 1), what drops (slide 2), the one product in focus (slide 3), last month's pick (slide 4), reminder CTA (slide 5).
- **Caption:** *"18 ko, shaam 6 baje. Reminder lagao, baaki hum sambhal lenge."*

## How I'd measure it

All **hypotheses**; I have no BSC baselines.

- **H1:** Habit-villain copy earns comparable or better click-through than rival-implied copy, with fewer compliance escalations. Watch CTR and comment sentiment. (Reduced legal exposure is a process outcome I can't promise as a metric.)
- **H2:** A recurring pre-sale content format raises traffic in the 18th 6-8 pm window and reminder opt-ins. Watch sessions in that window and reminder sign-ups.
- **H3:** The Uncle-duel reel holds viewers longer than a feature-led cut. Watch 3-second hold, completion rate and shares.

## Sign-off

*Wit needs a target. Make it the habit.*
