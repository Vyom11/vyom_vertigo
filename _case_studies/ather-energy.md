## What they're doing now

Ather's [homepage](https://www.atherenergy.com/) leads with a banner that says "Easy on Ather". Rizta is tagged "Made for your family", with a banner citing "4,00,000+ families"; the 450 is "The Ultimate Ride Experience"; the Halo helmet is "Pair your world". The calls to action are test-ride and finance links.

The big recent brand push is [It's Easy on an Ather](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/ather-energy-focuses-on-everyday-riding-in-new-brand-campaign-10992486) (Jan 2026). Per afaqs, the films show ordinary road moments in which AutoHold, Reverse Assist and SkidControl work without drawing attention. The in-house team made it, directed by Kishore Iyyar, and it ran six weeks across TV, digital and social, including the India vs New Zealand series and the Women's Premier League. A [Kyoorius entry](https://winners.kyoorius.com/winners/2026/kca/11536-story-in-a-frame) says seven ad films were made.

Before that came [All India Topper](https://www.medianews4u.com/ather-energy-launches-all-india-topper-campaign-to-celebrate-riztas-award-winning-debut/) (May 2025), a school metaphor presenting Rizta as a top student, with a film on Ather's YouTube channel and print ads. The article reports company claims of 13 major awards since the July 2024 launch; it does not verify them. And [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/indian-social-media-case-studies/how-hyperlocal-storytelling-shaped-ather-rizta-festive-family-scooter-positioning-10957532) wrote up a Rizta Dasara run (Dec 2025): three regional creators each weaved one Rizta feature into a Dasara ritual in humorous Instagram reels, with about 1.4 million views, an 8.61% engagement rate and Rs. 0.24 cost per view reported.

On [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/atherenergy/) (about 282k followers), the recent grid's captions show a counting contest built on Rizta's AutoHold (how many shopping carts can it hold), a post saying a rider crossed 1,00,000 km in under two years, a "If Ather were from the 80s" piece captioned as made by designers, not AI, a National Engineers Day greeting, voice-control and Community Day posts, and behind-the-scenes of a video in which wrestlers tested the Konarc. (Captions and thumbnails only, some truncated; I did not watch the reels.) On [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ather-energy/home/) (about 679K followers) recent items include a repost of the CEO's Q2 FY27 results note, a logistics-partner meet, an AI buildathon write-up, and hiring posts, one of which describes a creative team that argues over commas and celebrates a well-placed pause.

And the Rizta teasers: Ather [roped in The Great Khali](https://ackodrive.com/news/ather-riztas-seat-large-enough-for-the-great-khali/) for a "Sab se bada seat" teaser, and ran an [XXXXXXL seat OOH teaser in Mumbai and Ahmedabad](https://www.media4growth.com/campaigns/laqshya-media-group-executes-ather-rizta-xxxxxxl-seat-teaser-campaign-in-mumbai-ahmedabad-7332) (March 2024). CEO Tarun Mehta said of the seat: ["We ended up building the largest seat in the Indian market"](https://www.autocarpro.in/news/ather-rizta-launched-at-rs-110-lakh-to-woo-family-buyers--119953?amp=1).

## The gap

Credit first. Restraint is a hard sell in a category that shouts, and Ather's in-house team commits to it. The humour is anchored to a feature (a very large seat) and not to a celebrity cameo alone. That's craft.

Now the trouble, and it's one of rhythm. The feed is playful and the "Easy" films are quiet. The feed already turns AutoHold into a counting game, while the films, per press coverage, make the tech disappear. A campaign about technology you won't notice needs one thing the viewer does notice, and the two surfaces don't share it. (A reading, not a fact: I haven't watched the films.)

Second, many registers. Khali-sized humour, Mysore-specific festival humour, minimal "Easy" films, designer-made nostalgia and community posts all work on their own. The line that joins them (ease, for the family) is stated on the homepage but isn't obviously the thread across all of them. Hypothesis.

Third, a proven format shown in one place. The creator-per-ritual structure was written up for Karnataka's Dasara. It could travel to other states' festivals, wherever the dealer network supports it. That's hypothesis, not a claim about Ather's plans.

## The refinement

**Move 1: Give "Easy" a signature beat.**  
One micro-series, 15 seconds each, so the quiet films and the playful feed share a sound. Sample, "Reverse, Easy" (Reverse Assist is named in the coverage; I have not tested it):

- **(0-4s):** a narrow society lane, a scooter, a family member waving it back with both hands. Ambient sound only.
- **(4-11s):** the scooter reverses cleanly. The waving stops mid-gesture, unneeded.
- **(11-13s):** *(Beat. Full silence. Hold the frame.)*
- **(13-15s):** soft chime, white text: **"Easy on an Ather."**

The same one-second silence and chime close every film in the series, so the quiet becomes recognisable.

**Move 2: The festival-creator template, for another state.**  
A brief for three creators: one feature each, one ritual each, in their own dialect. If it ran around Navratri or Uttarayan, a caption in the brand's Hinglish:

> Seat badi, tension chhoti.  
> [Feature, one line, per creator.]

A Gujarati cut would be written natively, not translated from the Hinglish.

**Move 3: A report card, signed by owners.**  
Extend "All India Topper" by letting owners sign it. The feed already tells owner stories (the 1,00,000 km rider), so this makes it a series. A carousel of verified owner remarks, only with permission and with no invented quotes:

> **Slide 1:** Rizta ka report card. This time, owners mark it.  
> **Slides 2-5:** [Verified owner remark + the feature it's about.]  
> **Slide 6:** "[Confirm current owner figure]" families and counting. Book a test ride.

## How I'd measure it

All **hypotheses**; I have no Ather baselines beyond the figures Social Samosa reports.

- **H1:** The signature beat lifts unaided recall of "Easy" against films without it. Watch brand-lift recall and 3-second hold.
- **H2:** A second state's festival reels reach engagement comparable to the reported Dasara run. Watch engagement rate and cost per view versus that reported benchmark, and test-ride bookings by pin code.
- **H3:** Owner-signed report cards earn more shares and saves than a generic awards post. Watch shares, saves and test-ride link clicks.

## Sign-off

*Easy is the ride. Memorable is the job.*
