## The brief

A dedicated tea sub-brand account—part of an established regional food and beverage business—needed to break through the endless digital clutter of generic morning cup imagery, scenic tea-garden drone shots, and predictable lifestyle slogans. 

The social feed had plenty of product imagery, but virtually zero organic community conversation. The brief was to completely re-energize the brand's digital presence, transforming an ordinary FMCG product handle into an entertaining, highly shareable destination that daily tea drinkers would actively follow, tag friends under, and champion organically.

## The idea

Launch a full-funnel, character-driven campaign titled **"Chai Ke Rang"**: an irreverent, relatable personality report dissecting the daily rituals, fierce tribal rivalries, and unspoken neuroses of Indian tea drinkers across video reels, episodic carousels, and bite-sized social copy.

> **Plot twist:** written by a strict coffee person.

Instead of lecturing consumers on polyphenol counts or harvest elevations, the creative hook celebrated the human psychology of the cup: the afternoon office escape, the precise shade of milk-brown that dictates household peace, the impassioned ginger-vs-elaichi debates, and the existential dread of being handed lukewarm tea.

## What I did

- **Campaign Architecture & Content Leadership:** Conceived the strategic premise of "Chai Ke Rang" and served as account content lead, directing the narrative tone across video reels, swipeable carousels, and static community prompts.
- **Hook-Driven Reel Copywriting:** Scripted high-retention short-form video sketches built around immediate 3-second visual hooks, rapid comedic pacing, and razor-sharp observational dialogue.
- **Direction & Set Leadership:** Directed the short-form video shoots, choreographing camera movements, timing beats, physical comedy gags, and framing to maximize watch time and replay rates.
- **On-Camera Performance:** Performed as an actor in hero sketches, delivering authentic comic expressions and deadpan monologues that gave the brand a recognizable, charismatic face.
- **Carousel & Community Copy:** Authored punchy, swipeable personality cards categorizing distinct archetypes of tea lovers, complete with debate-sparking calls to action that invited followers to defend their personal brewing rituals in the comments.

## How it worked

Tea consumption in India isn't merely a beverage choice; it is an identity. By pivoting the brand's storytelling from "why our tea is premium" to "here is what your chai habits say about your soul", the campaign tapped into deep-seated cultural rituals:

1. **Tag-Your-Friends Psychology:** Every personality archetype in "Chai Ke Rang" was engineered to remind viewers of a specific colleague, parent, sibling, or roommate, sparking organic mentions and private DM shares.
2. **Polarizing Comment Prompts:** Debating tea temperature, boiling time, and sugar quantity generated fierce yet playful comment sections, driving algorithmic distribution far beyond the brand's follower base.
3. **Pacing and Retention:** Short-form video scripts kept scenes tight, punchy, and visually dynamic, ensuring audiences watched through to the closing brand punchline.

## The results

- **1M+** organic views generated across campaign reels and social assets.
- **25k+** likes and thousands of vibrant, opinionated community comments.
- Transformed the sub-brand's account into a lively, culturally attuned hub for beverage enthusiasts.
