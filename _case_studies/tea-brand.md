## The brief

A dedicated tea brand account—a sub-brand of an established regional foods conglomerate—needed to break through the saturated morning-beverage noise on social media and build a loyal, active community that shared content organically.

## The idea

A full-funnel, character-driven campaign titled **"Chai Ke Rang"**: an irreverent, relatable personality report dissecting the rituals, quirks, and unspoken philosophies of Indian tea drinkers across video reels, carousels, and bite-sized social copy.

> **Plot twist:** written by a strict coffee person.

## What I did

- Spearheaded account content leadership and developed high-hook copywriting tailored specifically for devout tea enthusiasts with clear community calls to action.
- Scripted, directed, and led end-to-end production for the short-form reel formats.
- Performed on camera in select hero sketches to nail comedic comedic timing, banter, and relatable daily chai struggles.
- Wrote punchy copy across static carousels and community engagement prompts to extend the campaign lifespan.

## How it worked

Instead of preaching about tea-leaf blends or mountain estates, the campaign focused entirely on the human psychology of chai consumption—the afternoon craving, the office tea-break politics, and the exact shade of brown that triggers household debates. Speaking directly to the ritual gave fans an immediate reason to tag their friends.

## The results

- **1M+** views generated across the brand account.
- **25k+** likes and organic community conversations.
