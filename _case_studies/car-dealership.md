## The brief

An authorized automobile dealership network in Gujarat needed to capture high-intent digital buyers during peak buying cycles, moving past generic corporate promotional templates with engaging performance copy and accessible video explainers.

## The idea

Pair sharp, conversion-focused pay-per-click search and display ads with high-energy, easy-to-understand reel explainers and seasonal festival hooks that demystify new automotive features and financing choices for everyday drivers.

## What I did

- Wrote performance pay-per-click (PPC) ad copy tailored for regional search intent and seasonal vehicle purchase queries.
- Scripted festive-season promotional reels connecting vehicle ownership to festive celebrations and family milestones.
- Created educational explainer reel scripts breaking down car specs, trim variations, and dealership benefits in conversational, jargon-free language.

## How it worked

Automotive buyers frequently feel overwhelmed by technical spec sheets and aggressive dealership sales tactics. Translating complex model features into everyday benefits while keeping copy crisp, relatable, and culturally attuned built immediate consumer trust.

## The results

- Content generated **2M+** views in total across the dealership network's video channels.
