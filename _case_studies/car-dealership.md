## The brief

An authorized automobile dealership network in Gujarat needed to capture high-intent digital buyers during peak buying cycles throughout the festive season, while standing out in a crowded market saturated with identical manufacturer-supplied promotional brochures.

Car buyers in the region were consistently overwhelmed by dense technical spec sheets, confusing trim levels, financing terms, and aggressive showroom sales rhetoric. The dealership needed an integrated copywriting approach: performance pay-per-click (PPC) ad copy to capture active searchers, paired with accessible, engaging video explainers that built trust before customers even walked onto the showroom floor.

## The idea

Bridge performance marketing with clear, conversational video education. Pair razor-sharp, conversion-focused pay-per-click search and display ads with high-energy, easy-to-understand reel explainers and seasonal festival hooks that demystify new vehicle features, ownership upgrades, and financing choices for everyday drivers and families.

By translating complex engineering terminology into clear, daily lifestyle benefits, we turned an intimidating purchase decision into an exciting, accessible family milestone.

## What I did

- **Performance PPC Ad Copywriting:** Authored conversion-focused search, display, and social pay-per-click ad copy tailored to high-intent regional automotive queries, test-drive bookings, and seasonal discount packages.
- **Festive-Season Reel Copywriting:** Conceived and scripted high-tempo promotional video reels connecting new vehicle ownership to family celebrations, festive milestones (Dhanteras, Diwali, Navratri), and road-trip aspirations.
- **Model Explainer Video Scripts:** Authored conversational video explainer scripts breaking down complex safety technology, infotainment systems, fuel-efficiency metrics, and cabin comfort into relatable, jargon-free consumer benefits.
- **Call-to-Action Optimization:** Crafted clear, low-friction conversion hooks that drove qualified viewers toward test-drive bookings, exchange valuation inquiries, and showroom walk-ins.

## How it worked

Automobile purchases represent one of the most significant investments an Indian family makes. Trust and clarity are paramount:

1. **Demystifying Tech Jargon:** Instead of listing engine displacements or electronic stability control acronyms, the video scripts explained what those features meant in real-world driving conditions—navigating monsoon waterlogging, weekend highway overtaking, or city parking crunches.
2. **Intent-Driven Copy Alignment:** Aligning the conversational tone of the video explainers with the precise search intent in the PPC ad headlines created a seamless consumer journey from initial search query to video watch to showroom lead.
3. **Festive Cultural Urgency:** Festive-season reel copy tapped into regional buying traditions, framing vehicle delivery not merely as a commercial exchange, but as a celebratory family milestone.

## The results

- Video campaigns and explainer reels generated **2M+** total views across the dealership's digital channels.
- Substantially increased organic brand recall and test-drive consideration across target regional territories.
