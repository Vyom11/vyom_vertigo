## The brief

A regional property-listing platform operating across Gujarat was battling an industry-wide crisis of audience apathy. Standard real estate marketing across India is notoriously sterile: cookie-cutter drone pans, generic stock music, repetitive square-footage numbers, and predictable amenity checklists that prospective home buyers swipe past in less than two seconds. 

The client needed a digital-first content overhaul that prospective tenants, first-time home buyers, and family decision-makers would actually stop scrolling to watch, comment on, and share across their family WhatsApp groups. The mandate was clear: stop treating listings like dry legal notices and start treating them like engaging video entertainment.

## The idea

Turn property discovery into cultural entertainment. Instead of measuring a prospective home strictly by carpet area and tile specifications, measure it by the practical, eccentric, and hyper-local priorities people actually debate when deciding where to live in Gujarat.

Homes are emotional and social hubs, not just architectural blueprints. By flipping the camera around and introducing high-hook formats that invited active audience participation, we transformed passive listing browsing into an interactive spectator sport.

## What I did

- **Content Strategy & Campaign Copywriting:** Conceived and authored the platform's overarching social video identity, replacing formal real estate marketing jargon with sharp, witty, conversational language.
- **"Crack the Price" Interactive Reel Series:** Conceived and scripted a game-show-style episodic reel series where viewers were given a high-speed tour of premium flats, penthouses, and suburban bungalows before being challenged to guess the exact price tag in the comments before the final on-screen reveal.
- **"Garba-Khush Property" Festival Campaign:** Conceived, wrote, and fronted a hyper-local Navratri campaign that completely disregarded traditional BHK counts and instead ranked properties based on their literal walking distance to the nearest major garba ground in the city.
- **On-Camera Performance & Presenter Lead:** Acted as the face and host across the video series, using comedic timing, physical expressiveness, and rapid-fire monologue delivery to keep viewer drop-off near zero.
- **Voiceover Production:** Wrote and recorded all voiceover tracks, delivering energetic, warm, and culturally rooted narrations for fast-cut property tour reels.

## How it worked

<!-- TODO(Vyom): confirm or edit this reasoning -->
By structuring property videos around curiosity gaps and cultural anchors, the content hacked the Instagram algorithm organically:

1. **The Guessing Mechanic:** "Crack the Price" gave viewers an immediate incentive to pause, re-watch, and argue in the comment section about market rates, neighbourhood valuations, and furnishing costs. This massive comment velocity signaled high engagement to the algorithm, propelling the reels onto regional explore feeds.
2. **Hyper-Local Cultural Resonance:** "Garba-Khush Property" tapped directly into Gujarat's greatest annual cultural obsession. Assessing a balcony not for its square footage, but for whether you could hear the dhol beats from the society ground, struck an immediate chord that made the videos instantly shareable among local families.
3. **Relatable Human Presence:** Replacing nameless corporate voiceovers with an expressive, quick-witted host transformed an impersonal digital listing directory into a trusted, approachable cultural friend.

## The results

- **1.7M+** organic Instagram video views generated across the campaign series.
- **40k+** likes and thousands of organic comments and price-guessing debates.
- Established the platform as a culturally attuned, entertaining market leader in regional property discovery.
