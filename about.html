About

The long version

I'm Vyom Pandya, an engineer by degree, a writer by habit and a cinephile by every other measure: 900+ films, 150+ novels, and 10+ years of theatre and painting. I've spent time inside an AI/ML team, so I know what a language model can and can't do. That's why my scripts and copy start with my own thinking, not a prompt: human-made, machine-literate.

I won first place in elocution at the 38th AIU Inter-University West Zone UniFest 2025, and I'm a sleight-of-hand magician—misdirection, timing and the reveal turn out to be a good copywriting education.
