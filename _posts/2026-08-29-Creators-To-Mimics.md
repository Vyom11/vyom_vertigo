I was recently reminiscing about the Ghibli trend that was all the rage a year back, and I looked at the global usage and found out that the only countries who created such images to their most extreme were India and Bangladesh. Then the recent trend of big-budget Bollywood films and their obsession with using AI videos into that, and I just find that so depressing.

The current trend of having everything be done with AI is so harrowing that even *Wall-E* would commit seppuku seeing what we are doing with the world, and that is something that I want to talk about this week.

Now I won’t be talking about the ecological, environmental effects of using AI, because let’s be honest. Until and unless our houses are the ones getting burned to warm someone, we don’t really feel the fire, so it’s anyway futile to give you that picture.

What I do want to talk about is the way we are consuming these LLMs. The biggest crime that people are doing using AI is that they are delegating their very act of thinking to these machines, and I find it fascinating because when you look at any great person, they have achieved what they have achieved because they did the action.

Carl Sagan rightfully said, “The brain is like a muscle. When it is in use we feel very good. Understanding is joyous.”

Honestly, I was this tempted to simply look through Google’s AI Overview and copy-paste from there, but I spent reading through the website BrainyQuote.com to find it. During the time, I also found many different wonderful quotes from different personalities. And the quote is a brilliant one at that because we have stopped using our brains for anything.

The only way to describe this is that with the way people’s thinking is getting, we are becoming expert mimics and not expert creators.

A few days back, Apple unveiled their new Mac mini M6, and the launch video was this wonderfully crafted stop-motion video. Within minutes, people created similar videos and posted them on Twitter (yeah, I’m not calling it X), saying how this cost so little, and how Apple wasted so much of their time, but that time, efforts was the point.

I miss the days when people would see such ads and try and create their own spin to it, in turn becoming expert creators at that, but now the people would see such ads, and spew that same thing, just with the tag of AI slop attached to it.

I go up to my office and see this kind of thinking practically everywhere.

A person uses ChatGPT to change the colour of a text, even though they are proficient in Photoshop, Illustrator, and many such editing tools.

Another example of a senior head of design whose first search is a simple Google search, but moving forward, it’s a complete continuation with the Google AI Overview, Gemini, chat.

How I never see one of the copywriters think anything on their own, and how one of the tabs that would be always open would be of ChatGPT and Claude, and how their entire day would be spent juggling between the two.

I am a culprit of the same, where I had gotten tired of thinking and simply asked ChatGPT to write a plot, refine the script, and more, but during the last couple of months, I myself have taken the conscious step to move away from my dependence on AI.

My scripts for reels, shorts, and YouTube videos are completely written by me. All the copywriting stuff I do involves reading, thinking, and then coming up with ideas, then refining them by looking up at different websites.

Now, that does make people think that I slack at my job, or how I spend time talking more than doing, but it’s only because of understanding these nuances of emotions that even if it takes time, my copies get approved with fewer revisions than my counterparts who use LLMs, where it does give you something resembling a good statement, but has none of the emotion, heart, and soul that would make a person remember your writing.

But hey, I am just a guy rambling on the internet, so you do you.

And I am especially weirded out by how normalised this is becoming and how no one is understanding the doom that lies ahead.

AI at its current stage can only look into the past, but for one to create, one must look into the future. And with the way the current diaspora thinks, I doubt whether we can look into the future.

Maybe that is because our necks are craned creating “art” on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude, while neglecting up on the actual art that exists.

I don’t want to look down upon anyone who calls them “AI Artist”; I am busy looking above at actual art, but don’t you think there is more joy when you learn a craft, try to implement it, fail at it, and finally be better at it, and when you finally upload it or even just look at it yourselves, the joy that you get, isn’t that what your humanity is all about?

But if you get that same pleasure by creating by creating AI flyers, then to each their own.

After all, some people go to eat a cuisine that is painstakingly made, and some simply enjoy pre-processed chips for dinner.
